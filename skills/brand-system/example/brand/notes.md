# Kitewell: notes

Kitewell is a made-up kite maker. This is the example that ships with the brand-system skill, and these notes show
what a brand's notes look like when the work is done. The dates and the owner are made up too.

## What was asked

- Who it is for: a small maker of kites, with a shop and a workshop where kites are built and mended.
- The name: Kitewell. The workshop is "Kitewell Workshop".
- What exists already: nothing. No logo, no colours, no fonts.
- Where it will be seen: YouTube and Instagram as @kitewell, a website, emails.
- Dark first. English only.
- It should feel light, made by hand and precise. Not a toy brand: no cartoon faces, no rainbow.

## Decisions

| Date | Decision | Made by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 May | The mark is a kite seen from the front, leaning into the wind, with a tail. Out of three directions (kite, spool, paper plane) | the owner |
| 2 May | The spars are gaps between four facets, not lines drawn on top | the owner, from a side option |
| 3 May | The lower right facet takes the colour. The tail stays white | the owner |
| 4 May | Breeze (teal to mint) for Kitewell, Poppy (red) for the workshop. Out of four accents | the owner |
| 4 May | Kitewell Sans, built from Raleway, with the name set in its Regular weight. Out of three typefaces | the owner |
| 4 May | A title font with diamond dots, since a diamond is a small kite. The dots are 66% of the round ones: at 90% the two dots of an umlaut touched | the assistant, shown and agreed |
| 5 May | The kite rises on the wind in its intro. In the loop nothing moves | the owner |

## Open

- The pattern art is quiet for this mark, because a tall mark is drawn small in a row. It was kept. It could be
  left out with `"art": { "skip": ["pattern"] }`.
- Whether the name and the kite are free to use was not looked into. That needs a trademark search.
